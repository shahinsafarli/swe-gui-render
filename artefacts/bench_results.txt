Command: python scripts/bench.py --n 5 --offline

Interpretation:
  - Sequential: awaits wiki → arxiv → web one after the other for each question
  - Concurrent: asyncio.gather(wiki, arxiv, web) per question, bounded by Semaphore(5)
  - The semaphore mirrors the production orchestrator (MAX_CONCURRENT env var, default 5)
  - Speedup of 2.6x with simulated latency (0.15s wiki, 0.20s arxiv, 0.18s web)
  - With real network (avg ~0.5–1s per source), concurrent speedup is typically 2–4x
